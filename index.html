Businesses with an annual turnover exceeding ₹40 lakhs (₹20 lakhs for special category states) are required to register for GST. Additionally, certain categories of businesses such as e-commerce operators, inter-state suppliers, and those making taxable supplies must register regardless of turnover. CA Niamul Khan can assess your specific situation and guide you through the registration process.

For individuals and HUFs not requiring audit, the due date is typically July 31 of the assessment year. For businesses requiring audit, it is October 31. For transfer pricing cases, it is November 30. Filing after the due date attracts late fees under Section 234F. It is advisable to file well before the deadline to avoid last-minute complications.

Commonly required documents include: PAN card, Aadhaar card, Form 16 (for salaried individuals), bank statements, investment proofs (for deductions under 80C, 80D, etc.), property documents (if applicable), capital gains statements, and Form 26AS / AIS. The exact requirements vary based on your income sources and applicable ITR form.

A CA provides invaluable support to startups through company incorporation, obtaining necessary registrations (GST, MSME, etc.), setting up accounting systems, ensuring statutory compliance, tax planning, and financial advisory. Early engagement with a CA helps startups build a strong compliance foundation and avoid costly errors that can arise from non-compliance.

Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is a mechanism where tax is deducted at the point of payment. Employers, businesses making payments above specified thresholds for services, rent, professional fees, etc., are required to deduct TDS. The deducted amount must be deposited with the government and returns filed quarterly. Non-compliance attracts interest, penalties, and disallowance of expenses.
